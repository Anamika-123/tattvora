# 🧘 Tattvora

> An AI-powered holistic wellness platform that combines Yoga, Ayurveda, Nutrition, Meditation, and intelligent coaching to deliver personalised wellness journeys.

Tattvora is an intelligent wellness platform that combines **Yoga**, **Meditation**, **Nutrition**, **Ayurveda**, and **Artificial Intelligence** to deliver personalized wellness programs for every individual.

Instead of offering one-size-fits-all classes, Tattvora understands each user's lifestyle, health goals, experience level, and wellness journey to provide personalised recommendations and continuous guidance.

----------

# 🌿 Vision

Our vision is to make holistic wellness accessible through technology.

Tattvora aims to become a complete wellness ecosystem where users can:
- Practice yoga from anywhere
- Receive personalized AI recommendations
- Track their wellness journey
- Connect with certified yoga teachers
- Improve long-term physical and mental well-being.
